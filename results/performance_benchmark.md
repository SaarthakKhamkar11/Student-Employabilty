## Conditions

- Machine: Linux 5.15.167.4-microsoft-standard-WSL2 (x86_64), 16 logical CPUs, 7.6 GB visible to the container, Python 3.11.17, torch 2.8.0+cpu (8 threads), embedding model `e5_base_v2` on CPU. Absolute numbers are specific to this machine; the relative order and the growth between sizes are what transfer.
- Production code, unchanged, against a throwaway Postgres + pgvector database per size (the live data untouched). Ingestion: one worker (1), every entity. Requests: 20 student, 10 upload and 10 company requests per size, seeded samples, one at a time, through the Supervisor as the portal sends them.
- LLM: none (deterministic routing for explicit tasks; structured explanation fallback). YouTube: disabled (curated catalogue only). The live service adds Gemini calls (routing for free questions, explanation prose) and YouTube searches, which this benchmark does not measure.
- Every stage latency comes from the Phase 12 trace spans the runtime records (agent, tool), summed per entity or request for stages made of several tools. A tool retry counts in the sum.
- Upload requests use plain-text resumes composed from stored rows (headings + the row's fields), so they exercise extract_text and parse_resume on realistic content but are not real uploaded files (no PDF extraction is measured).
- The E5 similarity calibration and the trained matching model are the production artifacts built on the real 286 x 4,990 data; they are reused unchanged at every size.
- **Tested ceiling: 5,000 students x 1,000 jobs** (the largest size run; the largest job corpus run is 4,990 jobs, at 286 real students). No claim is made beyond these.
- Bulk similarity is one numpy matrix product (median of 5) in a process that also holds the E5 model; at these sizes it takes milliseconds and its run-to-run noise exceeds the size effect, so read it as an order of magnitude only.
- Orchestration overhead (Supervisor request time minus the agents' critical path, the slowest agent of each concurrent wave; median per request): `real` student 4 ms, upload 4 ms, company 3 ms; `1k` student 4 ms, upload 5 ms, company 4 ms; `5k` student 4 ms, upload 4 ms, company 3 ms.
