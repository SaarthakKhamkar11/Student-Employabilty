## Limitations

- Training labels are weak (rule-based): every model learns the weak-label rules, including their leakage through branch_match and career_interest_alignment. Only human labels can show whether that generalises to real fit.
- Hyperparameters are fixed, not tuned, so a model's rank could change with tuning; tuning needs human-labelled training data that doesn't exist yet.
- Suitability scores are class-balanced probabilities: a ranking score, not a calibrated probability.
- The job corpus is synthetic and templated. Costs are from one machine.
- Quality is on the validation split only; test is held back for Phase 14.
