## How each baseline scores a pair

- **Baseline 1: normalized keyword matching** (`normalize_skills + aliases-42525be01cd6`): Student skills: the Profile Agent's stored-record path (record_skills_text -> extract_skills -> normalize_skills). Job skills: the Job Agent's phrase splitter (split_skill_phrases) -> normalize_skills. A job skill is matched when its normalised name equals a student skill's, or one contains the other as whole words. Score = matched job skills / job skills. No embeddings, no structured features.
- **Baseline 2: TF-IDF + cosine similarity** (`tfidf-953a75d166d8`): Phase 6 floor, src/embeddings/tfidf_baseline.py via the Phase 6 scorer (score_matrix, full_text protocol).
- **Baseline 3: structured feature matching** (`weighted_baseline_v1+545cea3bb767`): Phase 7's weighted_baseline_v1 (config/matching_baseline_v1.yaml) on the features-v2 E5 feature matrix, as the Phase 8 engine scores it. NOTE: its 20 features include 6 calibrated E5 similarity percentiles (weight 0.38 of 1.00), so it is not free of semantic signal.
- **Baseline 4: semantic embedding matching** (`intfloat/e5-base-v2@f52bf8ec8c71`): Phase 6's selected model (E5 e5-base-v2) alone, via the Phase 6 scorer (score_matrix, full_text protocol).
- **Baseline 5: hybrid semantic + structured matching** (`logistic_regression+a13440424c1f (artifacts mms-20261015T123632Z-nolabels, selected by mms-20261015T124013Z-human)`): The production Stage 4 model named by config/matching_model.yaml (the trained Phase 8 model, hash-checked) on the same features-v2 E5 feature matrix: E5 similarity features + structured features + the trained model.

Keyword coverage on the evaluated pairs: 28/30 students and 130/130 jobs have at least one normalised skill; 102 pairs share at least one skill. Pairs a baseline can't score rank last (counted as "unscored").

## Limitations

- 15 test students (180 pairs) and one labeller: most differences between neighbouring baselines are not statistically distinguishable, and this report says so per comparison.
- The labelling pool was built from the rankings of TF-IDF, SBERT, E5, BGE and MiniLM (TREC-style). B1, B3 and B5 did not nominate jobs. Condensed lists (only judged jobs are ranked) keep this fair to them, but none of the baselines is tested on jobs nobody nominated.
- Ranking judged jobs evaluates scoring (Stages 3-4), not Stage 1 eligibility or Stage 2 top-50 retrieval: a judged job the production pipeline would filter out still gets ranked here.
- B3 is not purely structured: 6 of its 20 features are E5 similarity percentiles (weight 0.38 of 1.00).
- The job corpus is synthetic and templated (376 distinct role texts). Results describe this data.
